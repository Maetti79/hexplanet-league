Build
Add floors to any dry plot once your camp has reached the level and you own the blueprint: timber from a wooden hut, stone from a stone house, concrete from a concrete house. Timber carries 2 more floors, stone 4, concrete 7. Neighbours on the same ground join into one house. The shovel takes the top floor down, paint colours the walls
Other gnomes
Twenty gnomes buy fields, chop, quarry, trade and build by the same rules. Their fields are protected. Economy shows where the tokens are; Speed ×10 or ×60 fast-forwards them and the clock
Nature
Now and then the ground rises into a volcano. Lava runs downhill, cools to rock, weathers to dirt and turns back into meadow over the following days. Forests seed themselves and young trees grow; some regions carry dense, dark forest. Thunderstorms bring lightning that sets forests on fire, and rain that puts fires out again. Lava, fire, lightning and flood water do not stop at your field: lava only runs downhill, so a trench catches it, concrete blocks it, and a camp on raised ground stays safe
Damage
Buildings take damage, turn sooty and lose their top floors. Your camp stays yours even as a ruin: click it with Camp to repair it one step at a time. Other buildings are repaired with Build
Camp
First buy a field. Then click the plot inside it for your tent; while it is a tent you can move it. Click the camp to upgrade it. Every house after the tent grows out of the shape of its plot, crooked like the ones you build yourself, and floors you build next to it at the same height join it. Stone houses and larger cover seven level plots
Trader
Click a market stall to buy tools and blueprints and to sell stone and wood
Wanderer
Once a game hour he buries a box if the last one was found and enough was going on. Look for freshly turned earth and dig

Deep water needs a boat, and a balloon flies over everything. A tailwind speeds both up. One game hour lasts 12.5 minutes; Time ×60 lets you watch a day pass.

Hex Planet

A small, unfinished world of crooked hexes. Dig, build, paint and reroute water. Whatever you change stays changed for everyone.
